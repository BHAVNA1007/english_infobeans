Back out = leave a plan 

Go ahead = start  

Take over = control 

Step down = leave a position 

Carry on = continue


Our team was planning a new project, but one member decided to back out at the last moment. The manager told us to go ahead with the project anyway. Then, our senior developer took over the project and guided everyone. After a few months, the manager decided to step down from his position. However, the team continued to carry on with the project and successfully completed it.
