## 🌱 Root Word: **“Scrib / Script”**

**Origin:** Latin **scribere**
**Meaning:** **to write**

Scrib (skrɪb)

### Words built from it

| Word            | Meaning                                                 | Example                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Describe**    | Write/tell what something is like                       | Can you **describe** the place?            |
| **Subscribe**   | Sign up/write your name to receive something            | I **subscribed** to the channel.           |
| **Prescribe**   | Officially write an instruction, especially by a doctor | The doctor **prescribed** some medicine.   |
| **Manuscript**  | A written document                                      | She submitted her **manuscript**.          |
| **Inscription** | Words written or engraved on something                  | There was an **inscription** on the stone. |
| **Transcript**  | Written record of spoken words                          | I received the interview **transcript**.   |

### 🧠 Easy connection

**SCRIB / SCRIPT = WRITE**

* **de + scribe** → write/tell **about** something
* **sub + scribe** → write/sign **under**
* **trans + script** → write **across/from one form to another**

**Best word to remember:** **prescribe** — *to officially write an instruction.*

> “The doctor prescribed a new treatment.”
