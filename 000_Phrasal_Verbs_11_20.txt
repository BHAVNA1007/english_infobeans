Phrasal Verbs 11–20

11
Look into — investigate — जाँच करना
Example: I will look into the problem.

Find out — discover/learn — पता लगाना
Example: I want to find out the truth.

Figure out — understand/solve — समझना / हल निकालना
Example: I finally figured out the answer.

Work out — solve / exercise / succeed — हल करना / व्यायाम करना
Example: We need to work out this problem.

Deal with — handle/manage — निपटना / संभालना
Example: She knows how to deal with difficult people.

12
Come up with — think of/produce an idea — विचार देना
Example: She came up with a great idea.

Carry out — perform/execute — पूरा करना / अमल करना
Example: They carried out the plan.

Set up — establish/arrange — स्थापित करना / व्यवस्थित करना
Example: He set up a new business.

Work on — spend time improving/doing — पर काम करना
Example: I am working on my English.

Give up — stop trying — हार मान लेना / छोड़ देना
Example: Never give up.

13
Run into — meet unexpectedly / encounter — अचानक मिलना / सामना होना
Example: I ran into my old friend.

Catch up with — reach same level / meet after time — बराबरी करना / हालचाल लेना
Example: I need to catch up with my studies.

Hang out — spend time casually — समय बिताना
Example: We hang out together on weekends.

Get along (with) — have a good relationship — अच्छे संबंध होना
Example: I get along with my classmates.

Part ways — separate/go different ways — अलग हो जाना
Example: After college, we parted ways.

14
Back out — withdraw from an agreement — पीछे हटना
Example: He backed out of the deal.

Go ahead — proceed — आगे बढ़ना
Example: You can go ahead with your plan.

Take over — take control — नियंत्रण संभालना
Example: She took over the company.

Step down — resign from a position — पद से इस्तीफा देना
Example: The manager stepped down.

Carry on — continue — जारी रखना
Example: Please carry on with your work.

15
Put off — postpone — टालना
Example: Don't put off your work.

Go through — experience / examine — गुजरना / जाँच करना
Example: I went through a difficult time.

Catch up on — do something missed — छूटा हुआ काम पूरा करना
Example: I need to catch up on my studies.

Keep up with — stay at the same level — साथ बनाए रखना
Example: I can't keep up with him.

Finish off — complete completely — पूरी तरह खत्म करना
Example: Let's finish off the project.

16
Come across — find/meet unexpectedly — अचानक मिलना / मिल जाना
Example: I came across an interesting book.

Look into — investigate — जाँच करना
Example: The police will look into the matter.

Dig up — discover/find information — जानकारी ढूँढ निकालना
Example: She dug up some old information.

Bring back — return / remind — वापस लाना / याद दिलाना
Example: This song brings back old memories.

Throw away — discard — फेंक देना
Example: Don't throw away the box.

17
Break into — enter by force — जबरदस्ती घुसना
Example: Someone broke into my house.

Get away — escape / leave — बच निकलना / चले जाना
Example: The thief got away.

Call off — cancel — रद्द करना
Example: They called off the meeting.

Look out for — watch carefully — सावधान रहना / ध्यान रखना
Example: Look out for cars while crossing.

End up — finally reach/become — अंत में होना
Example: We ended up staying at home.

18
Hold on — wait — इंतज़ार करना
Example: Hold on for a minute.

Hang up — end a phone call — फोन काटना
Example: Don't hang up the phone.

Call back — return a phone call — वापस फोन करना
Example: I will call you back later.

Put through — connect by phone — फोन पर जोड़ना
Example: Please put me through to the manager.

Cut off — disconnect / stop — काट देना / रोक देना
Example: The phone was cut off.

19
Take up — start a new activity — शुरू करना
Example: I took up Python last year.

Keep at — continue trying — लगातार प्रयास करना
Example: Keep at it and you will improve.

Give in — stop resisting — हार मान लेना
Example: Don't give in to pressure.

Drop out — leave school/course — पढ़ाई/कोर्स छोड़ देना
Example: He dropped out of college.

Carry on — continue — जारी रखना
Example: Carry on studying.

20
Come around — visit / change one's opinion — मिलने आना / राय बदलना
Example: She came around to my point of view.

Turn down — refuse / reduce — मना करना / कम करना
Example: He turned down the offer.

Bring along — bring someone/something with you — साथ लाना
Example: Bring along your notebook.

Join in — participate — शामिल होना
Example: Come and join in the game.

Head home — go home — घर की ओर जाना
Example: It's late, so I am heading home.

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCES

Find out — get information
Example: I found out the truth.

Figure out — understand/solve something
Example: I figured out the problem.

Look into — investigate
Example: The police are looking into the case.

Work out — solve / exercise / have a successful result
Example: We worked out the problem.

Catch up with — reach the same level/person
Example: I need to catch up with my classmates.

Catch up on — complete something you missed
Example: I need to catch up on my homework.
