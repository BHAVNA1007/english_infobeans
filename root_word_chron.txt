The root word **chron** comes from **Ancient Greek** and is related to **time**.

### 🔹 Root Word: **chron**

Origin:** Greek **khronos (χρόνος)**
Meaning:** **time**
Pronunciation:** *kron*
It appears in many English words related to **time, duration, or sequence.

### Common words with **chron**

| Word              | Meaning                                              | Example                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Chronology**    | The arrangement of events according to time          | *The book presents the chronology of the war.*                           |
| **Chronological** | Arranged in order of time                            | *Write the events in chronological order.*                               |
| **Chronic**       | Continuing for a long time                           | *He suffers from chronic back pain.*                                     |
| **Chronicle**     | A record of events in time order                     | *The historian wrote a chronicle of the kingdom.*                        |
| **Synchronize**   | To make things happen at the same time               | *Synchronize your phone with your computer.*                             |
| **Synchronous**   | Happening at the same time                           | *The two machines operate synchronously.*                                |
| **Asynchronous**  | Not happening at the same time                       | *Email communication is asynchronous.*                                   |
| **Chronometer**   | An instrument for measuring time accurately          | *The sailor used a chronometer to keep accurate time.*                   |
| **Chronograph**   | An instrument/watch that records or measures time    | *He bought a watch with a chronograph.*                                  |
| **Anachronism**   | Something placed in the wrong historical time period | *A modern smartphone in an ancient Roman movie would be an anachronism.* |

### 🧠 Easy way to remember

**CHRON = TIME**

Think:

> **Chronology → time order**
> **Chronic → lasting a long time**
> **Chronometer → measures time**
> **Synchronize → make things happen at the same time**

### 🔍 Word breakdown

**Chronological**

> **chron** = time
> **-logy** = study/account
> → **chronology** = the study/recording of events according to time

**Synchronize**

> **syn-** = together
> **chron** = time
> **-ize** = make
> → **synchronize** = make things occur at the same time

**Anachronism**

> **ana-** = against/back/wrong
> **chron** = time
> **-ism** = condition
> → **anachronism** = something that belongs to a different/wrong time period.

**Shortcut:** Whenever you see **CHRON**, immediately think **TIME**.
