1. Root word: ACT

Act comes from Latin agere, meaning:

“to do, to drive, to perform, to carry out”

The Latin noun actum means “a thing done” or “something performed.”

So the basic idea behind act is:

ACT → DO / PERFORM / TAKE ACTION



| Word           | Meaning                                | Connection to “act”                   |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **act**        | to do something                        | do                                    |
| **action**     | something that is done                 | act → action                          |
| **active**     | doing things; energetic                | someone who acts                      |
| **activity**   | something that someone does            | repeated/organized action             |
| **actor**      | a person who performs                  | person who acts                       |
| **actress**    | female performer                       | person who acts                       |
| **actual**     | real; existing in fact                 | something that has been done/existing |
| **activate**   | to make something work                 | cause something to act                |
| **activation** | the process of making something active | act → become active                   |

| **transact**   | to carry out a business/deal           | trans + act                           |
| **enact**      | to make something official/law         | en + act                              |
| **counteract** | to act against something               | counter + act                         |
| **inactive**   | not active                             | in + active                           |
