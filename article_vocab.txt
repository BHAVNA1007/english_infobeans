### Important differences

**1. Acute vs Continual**

* **Acute** = very severe/intense
  → *He experienced **acute pain**.*
* **Continual** = repeatedly happening
  → *Her **continual complaints** disturbed me.*

**2. Harmony vs Harmonise**

* **Harmony** = the state of being in agreement
  → *The team lives in **harmony**.*
* **Harmonise** = to create or achieve that agreement
  → *We should **harmonise** our plans.*

**3. Diminution vs Abate**

Both involve **becoming less**, but their usage differs:

* **Diminution** = a **reduction/decrease** (noun)
  → *There was a **diminution in** his influence.*
* **Abate** = something **becomes less intense** (verb)
  → *The storm **abated**.*

**4. Pursuit**

Usually used for something you are **trying hard to achieve**:

> **pursuit of knowledge** = ज्ञान प्राप्त करने का प्रयास
> **pursuit of happiness** = खुशी की तलाश
> **pursuit of a career** = करियर बनाने का प्रयास

**5. Threshold**

Think of a **doorway**. Figuratively, it means the point just before something new begins.

> *She is on the **threshold of success**.*
> = She is very close to entering a successful stage.

**6. Piecemeal**

Think: **piece + meal → one piece at a time**.

> *The project was completed **piecemeal**.*

= The project was completed **little by little / in separate parts**.
